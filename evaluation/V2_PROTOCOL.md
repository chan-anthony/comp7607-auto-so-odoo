# V2 protocol — local-first routing

Primary decision: among online policies meeting the wrong-draft target, maximize emails not sent to a hosted extractor. A local manual review is not a successful local draft: separately report correct local drafts per incoming email, correct-action rate, missed drafts, and review load. The unchanged execution gate and local/hosted candidates are shared across routing policies. Wrong-draft rate (WDR) = erroneous generated drafts / generated drafts, not erroneous drafts / all mail. Zero drafts gives undefined WDR.

Data: 150 calibration emails and 180 prospective evaluation emails in five held-out business families. Author texts, catalogue truth and action from business semantics, prior to model results. Keep related scenario variants together. Partition calibration by scenario into router fit and disjoint threshold calibration; never tune features or thresholds on the 180. If target headroom fails, record it. A revised collection gets a new version; a used panel is diagnostic, not a fresh blind test. Old 880 and native fixtures are known regression panels.

Model selection: fit regularized logistic regression on fit-only features and local candidate correctness. Orient all scores so larger means greater reason to escalate. Compare all-cloud, all-local, lexical, verbal, token uncertainty, raw Kev, disagreement-only and full hybrid. For active bypass paths, benchmark actual skipped computations. Calibration budget quantiles (20%, 30%) are online thresholds, not guaranteed deployment quotas; fix label-independent handling of ties. Report realized calls. Choose risk operating point maximizing local share under the calibration risk criterion; report risk and uncertainty on independent evaluation. Empirical calibration alone is not distribution-free certification on new business families. Keep offline exact-budget comparisons separate from deployment. Do not select operating points using the 880 known outcomes.

Latency: measure warm individual email wall time including extraction, routing, optional cloud, mapping and draft/review action; report mean and p95 and separate cold start. Compare on paired inputs. Record device, dtype, batch size and concurrency. Report verifier-only timings separately. No claim of <100 ms or negligible latency from GPU availability alone.

Reference feasibility on legacy stress, unchanged local/strong actions: the oracle fixes the two wrong local drafts that remain drafts first, then removes erroneous drafts. At 176 calls even the best possible WDR is 95/529; the first feasible strict sub-5% point is 249 calls (28.3%). At 248 calls it is 23/457. Local abstention would be a different action policy and must be reported separately.

Post-freeze engineering variant: after native regression at the 30% operating point, add explicit local manual-review reasons for instruction impersonation and existing-order tracking without a fresh dispatch. This is regression-driven remediation, not blind evidence on fixtures 18 and 20. Keep frozen hybrid as a separate comparator; count abstentions and missed orders.
### Authority-aware repair (development after first blind panel)
Separate the current sender from quoted text. Resolve unequivocal information-only/conditional messages to local review; accept a current unconditional dispatch locally only when both extractors agree on exact catalogue lines. Send unresolved cases to the trained risk scorer. For the conservative point, send unresolved cases to cloud whenever the frozen score meets its calibrated threshold. Keep manual review load and missed drafts visible. This is a new action policy, distinct from merely selecting a model. Any reused panel is diagnostic for this revision.

Deployment reference added after repaired challenge: cloud-confirmation routes explicit local-review cases to a human and every remaining case to the strong extractor. This is an engineering reference with no score fit, not a predeclared held-out operating point. It automates no drafts locally; count that distinction and report its held-out and legacy stress results as retrospective comparisons.
### Scoped authorization follow-up
The base is the previously frozen authority risk operating point; do not compare against a different call budget. Only two scoped overrides change: explicit reported/withdrawn-only language -> local review, and a root directive with completely consumed catalogue/positive quantity lines and an identified sender -> locally validated draft. All remaining cases use the unchanged Qwen/Kev/cloud route with identical cached conditions. Negative override may increase manual workload: count all final reviews, including cloud decisions, not just locally assigned reviews. Measure wrong/generated drafts (<5%), correct local drafts / all incoming and / all gold draftable emails, total reviews, missed drafts, actual cloud calls. Fixed paired synthetic challenge (5 families x 6 scenario scripts x 5 realizations). Examine scope-positive and scope-negative changes; no per-case selection or tuning after opening. Report "observed" rather than a population guarantee. No new compute is needed to replay the previously seen five.
## Third-outcome acceptance protocol
Keep the previous line-level quotation policy unchanged; compare the two variants on identical emails and cloud candidates. Every inbound email entering the source review queue or requiring human follow-up on an existing case or a new quotation counts once as human work. Verified ticket association alone never saves work when an action or reply remains. An FYI attached with no staff task is not a handled reply. Report missed-required-reply, missed-required-action, unmatched/incorrect links, wrong drafts/generated drafts, correct local drafts/gold draftable and cloud-exposed emails. Operational gold labels distinguish reply, staff action and none. The old panel has no verified case registry. Use a separate synthetic register on the new 160-case prospective panel and bind its entries to real native review case IDs during execution; this tests workflow behavior, not real support demand. No substitution of record IDs based on a gold decision. Freeze policy and manifest before inference.
